# Product

## Register

product

## Users

StateStreet leadership, department managers, and seven store managers use the
application during normal office and retail operating hours. Leadership scans
group performance and exceptions. Department managers review their source data,
approve work, and act on queues. Store managers complete a small number of
repeatable daily and weekly workflows, often while also running a store.

## Product Purpose

StateStreet turns retail operations data into one reliable operating picture.
It should make source data quicker to enter, prevent repeated or contradictory
facts, preserve an audit trail, and give the CEO an immediate understanding of
revenue, margin, targets, store performance, and issues requiring attention.

Success means a store manager can finish routine entry without retyping known
products or store details, a department manager can trust and progress the
result, and the CEO can make a decision without reconciling competing totals.

## Brand Personality

Assured, contemporary, and attentive. The application should feel premium
through precision, speed, and finish, not through luxury cliches or decoration.

## Anti-references

- The previous black and gold command-center identity.
- Flat grids of identical rectangular cards.
- Boxy layouts with hard visual stops around every piece of information.
- Generic black-and-white shadcn demos with no StateStreet character.
- Oversized marketing typography, decorative dashboard jargon, glass effects,
  and motion that delays work.
- Charts that look impressive but obscure definitions, periods, targets, or
  missing data.

## Design Principles

1. Ask once, derive the rest.
2. Put the decision before the decoration.
3. Use familiar controls and make state changes explicit.
4. Keep one definition for every metric across every role.
5. Earn a premium feeling through responsive feedback, calm hierarchy, and
   careful detail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, form labels,
error identification, and chart alternatives. Do not rely on color alone for
status or comparison. Respect reduced-motion preferences and preserve complete
workflows on compact laptop, tablet, and mobile viewports.
