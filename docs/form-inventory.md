# Form Inventory And Parity Contract

This document is the functional contract for the local rebuild. The current
JSON forms remain useful as evidence of what the business is trying to do, but
they are not the target data model.

## Rules For The Rebuild

- Ask for a fact once, at the point closest to where it originates.
- Derive totals, rates, weekly summaries, rankings, and dashboard metrics.
- Resolve stores, users, brands, categories, products, suppliers, payment
  methods, and expense categories from reference tables instead of free text.
- Prefill the signed-in user, assigned store, and sensible business date.
- Keep a draft when a workflow is long. Submit and approval are explicit state
  changes, not separate copies of the same data.
- Preserve the business outcome of every current workflow. A redundant form may
  disappear when its result becomes a derived view.

## Store Manager

### Daily store report

Replaces `Daily Sales` plus `Daily Closing Report`.

One report exists per store and business date. The store and manager come from
the session. Transaction, footfall, and customer counts are entered once on the
report header, not once per category.

Manual category fields:

- category
- opening stock
- units sold
- gross revenue
- COGS
- discounts
- credit sales

Manual closing fields:

- transactions
- footfall
- total, new, and returning customers
- amount per payment method
- optional note

Derived fields:

- net revenue
- gross profit and gross margin
- conversion rate, ATV, UPT, and repeat rate
- sales/payment variance
- daily, weekly, monthly, store, brand, and group totals

Workflow states are `draft`, `submitted`, and `approved`. Finance may reopen an
approved report only with a reason. Every change uses optimistic locking and an
audit event.

### Customer capture

Retained, but phone number identifies the customer so repeat visits update the
same customer rather than creating duplicate customer records.

Manual fields:

- name and phone
- lead or buyer
- source and optional source detail
- optional occupation and size preference
- optional product selection or free-text interest

The store, date, and captured-by user are prefilled.

### Stock transfer request

Retained as a typed inventory document. Products are selected from the catalog;
SKU, description, category, and unit cost are not retyped.

Manual fields:

- destination store
- one or more products and quantities
- reason and optional note

The source store, requester, and date are prefilled. Authorization and receipt
are separate workflow actions.

### Weekly review

Retained as a short management review. Weekly sales, units, opening/current
stock, targets, achievement, category performance, and ratings are derived from
daily reports, targets, and inventory data.

Manual fields:

- week ending
- risks and opportunities
- which category Marketing should amplify
- what the manager will do differently
- the manager's first three actions
- a performance comment for every active category assigned to the store; category
  cards are generated automatically and the existing card fields open on demand
- category-level risk flags where judgement is required; Stock at Risk is derived
  from current store quantity multiplied by the product catalogue's selling price
- action, owner, due date, unit/revenue target, and status

## Finance

### Daily report review

Replaces free-form revenue reconciliation. Finance reviews the whole store/day
report, sees payment variance and audit history, corrects an unlocked report,
and approves or reopens it. Finance does not create duplicate category entries.

### Expenses

Retained with typed expense category, optional store, amount, vendor, invoice
reference, payment method, description, and overspend reason. Budget position is
shown before save and calculated by the server.

### Budgets

Retained as one annual amount per expense category and optional store. Existing
rows are updated rather than duplicated.

### Capital, cash, working capital, and forecast

Retained as four focused typed workflows:

- annual capital employed and investment snapshot
- dated cash transaction for non-derived inflows/outflows
- debtor or creditor item with due date and status
- monthly financial forecast with confidence and assumptions

Cash generated by approved sales and typed expenses is derived. It is not
re-entered as a cash transaction.

### Excel import

Retained for expenses and budgets. Preview, row errors, conflict decisions,
batch audit, and undo remain part of parity. Imports write typed tables.

## Commercial

### Weekly sales and category performance

Removed as data-entry forms. They become read-only analysis derived from
approved daily reports, inventory, and targets. Markdown commentary remains an
optional commercial annotation where it cannot be derived.

### Product catalog and product insight

The current `New SKU Performance` form splits into:

- a product catalog workflow for SKU, variant, brand, category, cost, and price;
- a short product insight workflow for status, commercial performance, campaign,
  and judgement notes.

Units sold, current stock, sell-through, sales value, and days in stock are
derived when source data exists.

### Targets

Weekly store targets and executive monthly/annual targets are retained in one
typed target model. The metric, scope, period, value, and creator are explicit.

### Accountability

Consolidated into the shared action and target model. Names and roles use users
where possible; external owners may remain text.

### Weekly review oversight

Commercial can read every store review, comment, and manage actions without
re-entering the store's weekly figures.

## Inventory

### Product catalog

Retained as the SKU source of truth. Every size/color variant is a separate
product row. Products are selected everywhere else.

### Goods receipt

Retained as a supplier/store/date header with product quantity, unit cost,
condition, and discrepancy lines. Brand, category, description, and total value
come from the selected products and lines.

### Transfer

Retained as the same transfer workflow used by store managers, with broader
store access and authorization controls.

### Stock count

Retained as a store/date document with one line per product. Variance and stock
value are derived from system quantity, physical quantity, and unit cost.

### Replenishment

Retained as a store request with product lines, current quantity, requested
quantity, urgency, supplier, and review status.

### Dead stock

Removed as a duplicate manual stock form. It becomes a review queue derived
from product balances, value, and last movement. The user records only the
recommended action, justification, and resulting action status.

## Marketing

The following remain typed workflows because their source is human or an
external platform rather than another StateStreet form:

- campaign performance
- lead-channel summary
- social-channel metrics
- clienteling activity
- customer feedback
- action tracking

Brand and store selectors use reference data. Campaign, lead, and social rates,
ROAS, funnels, trend comparisons, and totals are derived.

## Operations

The following remain typed workflows:

- store standards review
- maintenance request
- visual-merchandising review
- staff-assessed store experience review
- incident report
- SOP review
- people snapshot

Recorded-by fields come from the session. Overall status, attendance, aggregate
scores, overdue state, maintenance totals, and corrective queues are derived.
Actions created from findings use the shared action model.

## Brand Health

The following remain typed workflows:

- brand health assessment
- brand sentiment snapshot
- competitor activity
- digital reputation snapshot
- customer feedback
- executive attention action

Overall health can be calculated from the component dimensions unless the
business explicitly records an override. Customer voice shares the customer
feedback table with Marketing. CEO attention shares the action table.

## Role Parity

- Owner: executive and every department dashboard, settings, users, targets;
  no routine data-entry forms.
- Finance: every main dashboard; finance forms and daily-report decisions only.
- Commercial: commercial dashboard and workflows.
- Marketing: marketing and brand dashboards and workflows.
- Operations: broad dashboards and cross-department data entry retained from the
  live role contract; approval ownership remains with the responsible department.
- Inventory: inventory dashboard and workflows.
- Brand: brand dashboard and workflows.
- Store manager: assigned-store dashboard and store workflows only.
